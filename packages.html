Solar Packages for Different Energy Needs

Start with a package that is close to your needs, then let iSCOOP confirm the final configuration. Appliance examples below are indicative, not guaranteed runtime commitments.

Before you choose

Actual performance depends on appliance wattage, start-up surge, simultaneous use, battery state, usable depth of discharge, inverter efficiency, solar availability and site conditions. A load assessment is required before final system confirmation.

May support one efficient small inverter AC for limited periods after a load assessment; do not combine with other heavy loads without validation.

Strong inverter capacity with modest battery storage; ideal for daytime productivity and managed evening loads.
