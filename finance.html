05

We confirm receipt and explain the expected next step. Receipt is not an approval or credit decision.

06
Suitable provider matching

Where appropriate, iSCOOP routes the enquiry to suitable provider(s) based on system value, profile and provider criteria. Each provider makes its own decision.

What may affect approval
  • Employment/Business Profile

    Shows how stable and reliable your source of income is.

  • Income/Turnover

    Shows whether you earn enough to repay the loan.

  • Credit Assessment

    Reviews your past borrowing and repayment behaviour.

  • Package Value

    Checks whether the amount being financed matches your financial capacity.

  • Customer Contribution

    The amount you are expected to pay upfront from your own funds.

  • Requested Tenor

    The repayment period and whether the monthly payment is affordable.

  • Provider Policy

    The lender’s internal rules and eligibility requirements for approval.

Eligibility & documentation

Prepare for a provider assessment.

A finance provider may ask for identity and contact information, evidence of income or business turnover, and details of the selected system or quotation. Requirements vary by provider and applicant; the provider will confirm the exact documents and assessment steps.

Submit documents only through the channel specified by the finance provider. iSCOOP does not publish or guarantee lender criteria.

Finance disclaimer

Finance is subject to eligibility, credit assessment and partner approval. Interest, fees, contribution and tenor vary by applicant and provider. iSCOOP does not guarantee credit approval.
